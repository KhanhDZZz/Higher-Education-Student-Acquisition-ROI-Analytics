# Higher Education Student Acquisition & Marketing ROI Analytics

## Executive Summary
This project presents an end-to-end Marketing Performance & Student Acquisition Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI. The objective is to evaluate campaign efficiency, conversion funnel metrics, lead acquisition costs, and geographic performance across multi-channel digital marketing efforts (Facebook, Google Ads, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube) for higher education admission programs (UG, MBA, Exchange Programs, etc.).

Through a total spend of **$91.45M**, the campaign generated **$2.17B in total revenue**, achieving an overall **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 23.68x** and an average **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of $233.10** across **392K total enrollments**.

- **High-Yield Return:** The omnichannel strategy achieved an impressive **23.68x ROAS**, demonstrating high capital efficiency in driving enrollment revenue.
- **Seasonality & Revenue Peaks:** Revenue and ROAS exhibit strong seasonal spikes aligned with major enrollment cycles:
  - **Q1/Q3 Peaks:** Significant peaks in **March & August 2024** and **May 2025** (ROAS reached ~28x).
  - **Q4 Dips:** Performance dips during October–November, with ROAS dropping to the 18x–20x range.
- **Platform Revenue Contribution:**
  - **Google Ads** and **Facebook** served as primary revenue drivers, contributing **23.57%** ($531.97M) and **22.92%** ($517.28M) to total revenue, respectively.
  - **LinkedIn** contributed **20.59%** ($464.61M) in revenue while maintaining a disproportionately low ad spend share (0.81%), signaling high ROI potential.

### 2. Conversion Funnel Efficiency (Page 2)
<img width="1952" height="1095" alt="FUNNEL   CONVERSION ANALYSIS" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2b869e2a-0f91-47e4-add4-eb482f65a1ea" />

- **Funnel Drop-off Analysis:** Out of **7M total clicks**, the acquisition funnel achieved:
  - **Click-to-Lead Conversion:** 19.91%
  - **Lead-to-Application Conversion:** 55.13%
  - **Application-to-Enrollment Conversion:** 50.06%
  - **Overall End-to-End Conversion Rate:** 5.50%
- **Funnel Bottleneck:** Downstream funnel stages (Lead-to-App and App-to-Enrollment) operate at high efficiency (>50%), indicating a strong sales/counseling nurture pipeline. The primary conversion bottleneck resides at the **top-of-funnel (Click-to-Lead at 19.91%)**.

### 3. Campaign, Channel & Creative Efficiency (Page 3)
<img width="1953" height="1101" alt="CAMPAIGN   CHANNEL EFFIENCY" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5e36392b-6863-4294-a623-e7641690629a" />

- **Platform Cost-Efficiency Matrix (ROAS vs. CAC/CPL):**
  - **LinkedIn** emerged as the top-performing acquisition channel, delivering the **highest ROAS (25.45x)** and the **lowest CAC ($218.61)** & **CPL ($62.50)**.
  - **Google Ads** and **YouTube** exhibited higher acquisition costs (CAC of $242.74 and $243.38, respectively), pulling down channel-specific ROAS to ~22.13x–22.97x.
- **Campaign Benchmarking:**
  - The **Placements** campaign demonstrated optimal cost efficiency (25.45x ROAS).
  - The **Hackathon** campaign was the most expensive acquisition vector (CAC $243.38, CPL $66.14).
- **Creative Type Performance:**
  - **Carousel Ads** delivered the lowest unit acquisition costs across both CPL and CAC metrics.
  - **Static Ads** incurred the highest acquisition cost per user (~$245 CAC).

## Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

1. **Reallocate Budget to High-Efficiency Vectors:** Shift budget allocation toward **LinkedIn** and **Carousel Ad Formats** to capitalize on lower CPL/CAC metrics and maximize net ROAS.
2. **Optimize Top-of-Funnel Experience:** Focus CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) efforts on landing pages and lead forms to improve the initial **Click-to-Lead conversion rate (19.91%)**, leveraging the strong downstream nurture pipeline.
3. **Double-Down on Core Demographic:** Maintain primary media focus on the **17–21 demographic**, refining ad messaging to match undergraduate admission timelines.
